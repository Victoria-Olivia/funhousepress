Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Fun House Press respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected or processed when you visit the Fun House Press website.

This website is intended primarily for visitors in the United States.

Information You Provide

Fun House Press does not currently require visitors to create accounts, provide names, submit email addresses, or provide payment information through this website.

Free downloadable materials available through the website may be downloaded directly without providing personal information.

Google Analytics

Fun House Press uses Google Analytics to better understand how visitors use the website. Google Analytics may collect information about website activity, such as pages viewed, approximate geographic location, browser or device information, session information, and interactions with the website.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to distinguish visitors and sessions. Information collected through Google Analytics is processed according to Google's own privacy practices.

Fun House Press uses this information for purposes such as understanding website traffic, improving website content, and learning which pages or features visitors find useful.

Cookies

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies to help measure website usage and distinguish visitors or sessions.

You may control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how certain websites or services function.

GitHub Pages Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically log technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for security and operational purposes.

Information collected by GitHub is governed by GitHub's own privacy practices and policies.

Third-Party Links

Fun House Press contains links to third-party websites and services, including Amazon and social media platforms.

When you follow a link to another website, that website may collect information according to its own privacy policy. Fun House Press does not control the privacy practices, content, or data collection activities of third-party websites.

Amazon Purchases

Fun House Press does not process purchases directly through this website. Books displayed on this site may link to Amazon, where purchases are completed.

Any payment, shipping, account, or transaction information provided to Amazon is handled by Amazon and is subject to Amazon's own terms and privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Although Fun House Press creates coloring books and creative materials that may be enjoyed by children and families, this website does not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's internet activity and use of external websites.

Data Sharing

Fun House Press does not sell personal information collected directly through this website.

Technical or analytics information may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google and GitHub as described in this Privacy Policy.

Your Privacy Choices

You may adjust your browser settings to limit or delete cookies. Google also provides tools that allow users to opt out of certain Google Analytics tracking.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Fun House Press may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, technology, or applicable practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to Fun House Press through official Fun House Press social media channels.
